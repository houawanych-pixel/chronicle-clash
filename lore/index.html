Chronicle Clash

Lore and Stories

Chronicle Clash is an original anthology of connected worlds. Each world arc can feel like its own classic fantasy setting, but they're all ultimately linked by the Clash. Its stories are cinematic and emotional, with themes of love, loss, war, identity and sacrifice.

The Grand Festival

The Grand Festival begins as a celebration of peace. Then a dormant Guardian awakens, the sky turns red, and the festival falls into crisis. The story grows from there into a wider conflict across regions and beyond.
