Controls & goals

Phone: left stick moves, right stick turns the camera. Use the action buttons. Landscape recommended.

Desktop: WASD move · arrows / drag view rotate · T lock / switch target · Space charged attack · C crouch · E cover · hold Q peek · Shift dodge · G summon · F climb / drop · Esc pause.

Near enemies, basic sword attacks are automatic. Keep a visible target locked to fill up to six dots; Attack releases one hit per full dot. Climb with forward/back. Attack the red core from the hostile colossus's shoulder.
